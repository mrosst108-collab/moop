# H2: counterfactual corners, frozen before execution

Q is unchanged (49 questions, `README.md`). Baseline stands at 45/49
(`baseline.md`). H2 is a method evaluated against the same Q: can
executing X/θ combinations that history never instantiated answer what
inspection could not, and turn reasoned answers into observed ones?

## Operational definitions

**X (state, source).** The contents of `cJSON.c` and `cJSON.h` at a
commit, taken verbatim. Where a header defines a parameter under an
`#ifndef` guard, the guard is source; the *value* it defaults to is θ.

**θ (parameters).** Two components, both taken from the commit's own
files, never authored:

1. *macro defaults*: every `#ifndef NAME / #define NAME value` pair in
   `cJSON.h` — the value component;
2. *flags*: the `R_CFLAGS` line of the commit's `Makefile` if one exists,
   with `-Werror` removed (a diagnostic must not abort a corner) and make
   variables dropped; applied to compiling `cJSON.c` only. The probe is
   always compiled the same way and linked against that object.

**The intervention.** The system's own knob: a guarded macro accepts a
command-line `-DNAME=value`. Setting θ on a source that reads the macro
uses that knob and edits no source. Setting θ on a source that does not
read the macro is inert — and that inertness is an observation, not an
assumption.

**The one assumption, disclosed.** For a macro that exists at E but not
at P, "θ_old" must say what value stands for the mechanism's absence.
Rule: the identity of the comparison the source diff shows the macro
used in. `depth >= LIMIT` → `LIMIT = SIZE_MAX` (never fires);
`fabs(a−b) <= TOL` → `TOL = 0` (reduces to equality, the previous check).
The comparison form is read from the record (the diff); the identity is
the value under which the new branch is unreachable or equal to the old
code. Any Q4–Q6 answer that rests on this rule is marked **(identity)**
and scored answered only if the corner run produces a discriminant; if
the rule itself is contested, those answers fall back to unanswered.

## Corners, per event E with predecessor P

    (X_P, θ_P)   sanity: must reproduce record.tsv at P
    (X_E, θ_E)   sanity: must reproduce record.tsv at E
    (X_E, θ_P)   E's source, P's flags, and for every macro whose default
                 is new or changed at E: -DNAME=<P's value, or identity>
    (X_P, θ_E)   P's source, E's flags, and -DNAME=<E's value> for the
                 same macros (inert if X_P does not read NAME)

A corner that fails to compile is recorded NOBUILD and answers nothing.

## Mapping to Q, frozen

Per facet that changed at E (Q1 from the record):

- **Q4** yes iff `(X_E, θ_P)` shows the facet's *new* value.
- **Q5** yes iff `(X_P, θ_E)` shows the facet's *new* value.
- **Q6** yes iff `(X_E, θ_P)` and `(X_P, θ_E)` differ on the facet — the
  two orders pass through different intermediates; this is the observed
  commutator, γ ≠ 0.
- **Q7** names any corner that is NOBUILD or rests on (identity).

Q1–Q3 are answered from the record as the baseline did; the method adds
nothing there and claims nothing there.

## Offices used, and not

This method uses the X/θ separation, F (a parameter change as a
distinct act), and γ (the observed commutator). J♯, G♯, G̃♯, Σ, κ are not
instantiated: the target does not present them, and none is added for
completeness (`../prompts/asdg-rme7.md` §7).

## Preregistered outcomes

- 4/4 of the baseline's unanswered questions answered by a discriminant:

- Some: a narrower domain of usefulness, to be described exactly.

## Instrument corrections (after the freeze, before adjudication)

The intervention above is unchanged since `f4fa8ba`. Three defects in the
*instrument* were found and corrected; none touches a corner definition,
a probe input, or the scoring rule.

1. *Display truncation* — the corner summary cut off before the depth
   facets. Presentation only; the underlying output was complete.
2. *Hash serialization* — the first, uncommitted `corners.sh` hashed the
   output without its trailing newline, so corners could not be compared
   to `record.tsv`. Corrected to hash the way the sweep does.
3. *Shell job message on a crash* — E1's historical commit aborts with
   heap corruption. Under `sh` the shell's own "Aborted" line entered the
   captured stream; under `bash`, which produced the frozen record, it did
   not — so the committed `sweep.sh` would not have reproduced its own
   record under `sh`. Corrected in both scripts with `timeout
   --preserve-status`, which exits normally and provokes no shell message,
   and by computing every corner's hash with the sweep's exact expression
   (a direct pipe into `md5sum`).

Validation after correction: `sh sweep.sh` reproduces `record.tsv`
**386/386 rows** byte for byte; all fourteen sanity corners `(X_P,θ_P)` and
`(X_E,θ_E)` reproduce the record's hashes exactly, E1's crash included.
The mixed-corner hashes are identical to those first recorded at
`92b6231`; the corrections changed no result.

One caveat kept on the record: after heap corruption the probe's byte
stream is undefined behavior. Under the frozen capture mechanism it is
reproducible (three shells and runs agree), and that mechanism is the
evidence; the display-only text of a crashing corner may differ from it.
The facet at E1 is the abort itself, and every E1 answer rests on crash
versus print, not on crash text.

Four things kept distinct: the *recorded* historical corner (the sweep,
empirically instantiated); the *reproduced* historical corner
(validation of the instrument, above); the *mixed* corner (counterfactual
execution); and the *attribution conclusion* (an inference from the
mixed corners' observations, in the tables below). The fourth is never
promoted to the third.

## Results

`corners.sh` run on all seven events; sanity corners verified as above.

| event | `(X_E,θ_P)` | `(X_P,θ_E)` | Q4 | Q5 | Q6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | E's behavior | P's (crash) | yes | no | yes (differ) |
| E2 | E's (`str` REJECT) | P's — the new flags are inert, **observed** | yes | no | yes |
| E3 | E's | P's | yes | no | yes |
| E4 | E's (`1.5`) | P's | yes | no | yes |
| E5, number facets | new numbers | P's | yes | no | yes |
| E5, depth facet | **ACCEPT** at 1001 (identity) | ACCEPT (inert) | **no** | **no** | **no** — both intermediates accept; γ = 0 |
| E6 | P's exactly — tolerance 0 reproduces the old check (identity) | P's (inert) | **no** | **no** | **no** — γ = 0 |
| E7 | 15 digits (new formula, old value) | 17 digits (old formula, new value) | no | yes | yes — γ ≠ 0 |

Two things the corners show that history does not contain. `(X_E,θ_P)`
at E5 is a behavior that never existed: hash `747d03f0b093`, absent from
`record.tsv` — the new number printing with no nesting limit. And E5 and
E6 are *joint* changes with γ = 0 (neither side alone produces the facet
change, and the two orders pass through the same intermediate), which is
a different thing from E7, a *parameter* change with γ ≠ 0. The
baseline's Q6 could not have separated those two shapes; the corners do.

## Score

| | A / 49 | Q4–Q6 answered / 21 |
|---|---|---|
| baseline (`baseline.md`) | 45 | 17 |
| H2 corners | **49** | **21** |

    ρ_rel = (49/49) / (45/49) = 49/45 ≈ 1.089 — the ceiling under this rubric.

The four recovered questions are E5 Q4, E5 Q6, E6 Q4, E6 Q6. All four
rest on the identity rule and are marked so; in each, the corner run
produced a discriminant — `(X_E, LIMIT=SIZE_MAX)` differs from
`(X_E, LIMIT=1000)` on the depth facet, and `(X_E, TOL=0)` differs from
`(X_E, TOL=.00001)` on the precision facet — so the mechanism demonstrably
reads the parameter, and the answer is observed, not assumed. If the
identity rule is contested at adjudication, the score falls back to 45
and the method's gain is exactly zero.

Separately, not scored: the corners confirm all 17 of the baseline's
reasoned Q4–Q6 answers and contradict none. Two move from assumption to
observation — E2's "warning flags are diagnostics" (the flags are inert
on the probe) and E7's arithmetic about `%1.15g` rounding.

## What this establishes, and what it does not

Established: a specific capability — attribution recovered by executing
X/θ combinations absent from history, on the two events where inspection
had nothing to inspect, with a disclosed assumption and an observed
discriminant. Preregistered outcome: 4/4.

Not established: that RME-7 beats ordinary tooling in general. The ceiling
was 1.089 because Q rewarded what git already does; the whole gain is in
four questions of one shape. The method used the X/θ separation, F, and
γ; four offices were unused and are not counted against it.
